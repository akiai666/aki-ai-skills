# Content Contract

## The Reader's Four Questions

The HTML must answer these in order:

1. Is this video worth my time?
2. What is the full line of thought?
3. Which exact moments should I watch?
4. What can I reliably keep or act on?

## Required Layers

### Quick Preview

Concise judgment with topic, conclusion, audience, watch-worthy moments, and
reading time. Every watch-worthy moment must be a timestamp link. The section
must remain useful even if the reader stops here.

### Highlight Map

Six to twelve selected moments. Each item has a type, title, explanation,
timestamp, and preferably a meaningful frame. Highlights are editorial choices,
not evenly spaced chapters.

### Reading Article

Five to ten sections of connected prose. Preserve the original argument,
examples, and context. A paragraph should express one idea and normally remain
under 160 Chinese characters.

### Verification

Quotes, evidence, disagreements, caveats, full chapter navigation, source URL,
and a searchable timestamped transcript.

## Type Adaptation

| Type | Lead with | Do not lose |
|---|---|---|
| interview | people and positions | disagreement, stories, speaker attribution |
| tutorial | outcome and sequence | demonstrations, pitfalls, prerequisites |
| business | thesis and evidence | numbers, scenarios, risks, uncertainty |
| talk | argument and concepts | analogies, cases, implications |

## Source Discipline

- A core claim without a timestamp is incomplete.
- A quotation without an exact source segment is not a quotation.
- A number spoken in the video is a source claim, not independently verified.
- A prediction must be labeled as a prediction.
- An agent inference must be phrased as interpretation.
- When transcript wording is uncertain, retain the uncertainty in `caveats`.

## Screenshot Discipline

Prefer frames that show:

- a chart, slide, diagram, tool, place, object, or demonstration;
- a speaker change relevant to attribution;
- a visual turning point that the prose discusses.

Avoid duplicate talking-head frames. The frame timestamp should fall inside or
near the associated highlight or article section.

## Writing Style

- Clear, restrained, and concrete.
- Connected paragraphs over lists.
- Bold only the true takeaway within a paragraph.
- Quote cards are for exact memorable language, not generic slogans.
- Keep titles descriptive; avoid sensational rewrites.
